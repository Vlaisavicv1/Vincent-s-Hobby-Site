The Beginning...

On the cold world of Patrician IX a revolutionary party called the Aracadian freedom initiative arose after a two year worker's strike that resulted in several hab complex wide clashes with the Arcadian Enforcers. The particular event that turned a flashpoint into a full revolution took place after half of the Planetary Defense Force flipped towards the AFI and flooded the ranks with trained infantry and APCs. The planetary governor called a state of emergency and requisitioned aid from Patrician system planets. The war for Patrician IX had well and truly begun.

A long stalemate

Elements of the 23rd Patrician V Dragoons responded to the requisition, nearly a regiment of Imperial Guardsmen were placed under the direct command of the planetary governor. Operations to take back the streets of the capital Neo Arcadia took place. Street by street fighting with AFI only emboldened rebel forces. The 23rd Patrician V Dragoons found themselves surrounded and outnumbered often. For five years this would continue until the planetary governor would once again request more aid to Patrician IX, this time to the Adeptus Administratum.

It would take well into a half standard terran year for the requisition to fall on anyone notable within the Administratum's desk and the request itself was kicked around the halls of sector admin before it finally fell on a scribes desk and was approved. A regiment of Mordian Iron Guard would be sent to the planet. Alloted two years to solve the issue and a promise that any more aid would certainly become a matter of whether ot not the planetary governor would maintain his position. The planetary governor was most definitively on the clock. Three months of transit time and plans were already in place for an offensive in the capital city.

The Imperial offensive opened with a salvoes launched from the newly acquired Mordian Iron Guards artillery pieces. To the Northwestern Quarter of the city the fighting opened. So intense was the fighting that this region of Neo Arcadia would become 'The Hellmire' fought over in some of the last actions of the Fengorn Operations. The offensive would regain control of the quarter of the city and give room for further offensives into the mostly rebel held South. While the gain itself was a forgone conclusion, the high effort; low gain for the Imperial forces was notable. Moreover, the AFI had seemingly found room for a counteroffensive to the Southeast with an attack on the PDF Valkyrie starport. Destroying nearly three promethium storage tanks in the process. It was only by mass expenditure of the remaining Patrician V Dragoons that the Valkyrie Port was held. Pulling them off the roster for the force.
